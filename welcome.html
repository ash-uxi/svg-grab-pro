Grab any SVG with a right-click

Right-click an icon, logo or illustration on any website to copy it, or save it as an SVG file.

To do that everywhere, SVG Grab Pro needs your permission to run on the websites you visit. It only looks at a page when you right-click on it or open SVG Grab Pro, and it doesn’t collect anything about your browsing.

Your browser will ask you to confirm. Its warning sounds broad because it covers every website.

Not now? Click SVG Grab Pro in your browser’s toolbar or extensions menu, and right-click works on that page until you leave it. You can turn this on there later, too.
