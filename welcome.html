Right-click any SVG

To add Copy SVG and Download SVG to your right-click menu on every site, SVG Grab Pro needs access to the sites you visit. It only looks at a page when you right-click or open it.

Not now? Open SVG Grab Pro on a page and right-click works there until you leave. You can allow all sites later from its settings.
